Giving SSD industry outreach a clearer structure

The department structure I have been planning for sponsorships and speaker relationships.

Danny Perojevic · October 13, 2026· Updated October 15, 2026

Separate sponsorships from speakers

I’ve been planning an Industry Relations department for SSD. The structure has two teams under one department head: Sponsorship & Finance, and Industry Speakers. Both need outreach, but I do not want their responsibilities to blur together. A sponsorship conversation and a speaker invitation have different next steps.

Sponsorship & Finance

The plan gives this team a lead and three officers. I emphasized persistence when writing the responsibilities. Someone has to send the first email, follow up, try another contact, and keep going after rejection. The role needs people who will do that work consistently, rather than wait for a company to approach us.

Industry Speakers

The speaker team would have a lead and two officers. I care about how we build rapport with people in the industry. An invitation should explain why their experience would be useful to our students. I want the team to keep track of those conversations so the next officer has more to work with than a name and an email address.

What I’m still figuring out

Writing the roles is the first part. The next part is deciding how the teams share progress and hand off contacts without making every task depend on the department head. This is the structure I’ve been developing with SSD. Specific speakers and sponsorships will come from the outreach itself.
